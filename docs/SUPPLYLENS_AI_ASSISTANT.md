# SupplyLens AI Assistant User & Operator Guide

## 1. Overview
The SupplyLens AI Assistant is an interactive sourcing consultant designed for entrepreneurs and procurement teams. It translates complex manufacturing specifications, regulatory requirements, and logistics variables into clear, actionable advice grounded in real supplier data.

---

## 2. Key Capabilities
- **Verified Supplier Discovery:** Instant lookup of factories, certs, and operational scale based on prompt queries.
- **BOM & Material Specifications:** Explains material grades (e.g., molded sugarcane bagasse vs. PLA coating) and technical requirements.
- **Landed Cost & Freight Analysis:** Analyzes ex-factory price vs. road freight, port handling, and GST tariffs.
- **MOQ & Working Capital Strategy:** Guides founders on negotiating tiered pilot orders to preserve cash flow.
- **Formal RFQ Generation:** Generates standardized, ready-to-dispatch Request for Quotation documents.
- **Side-by-Side Comparison:** Compares suppliers in structured markdown tables.

---

## 3. UI Interfaces
1. **Persistent Floating Launcher:** Located at the bottom-right corner of authenticated pages with real-time status indication.
2. **Interactive Chat Sheet:** Allows chatting without losing context of the active page.
3. **Dedicated Full-Screen Workspace (`/assistant`):** Features conversation history management, search, and Markdown report export.
