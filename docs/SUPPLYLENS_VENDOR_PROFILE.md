## 1. Executive Summary

The **Vendor Detail Page** is designed to be one of the most comprehensive and authoritative interfaces in SupplyLens. When an entrepreneur or procurement officer opens any vendor, they receive complete, unadulterated operational intelligence across 10 deterministic dimensions, product lines, landed pricing, physical facilities, and legitimate contact channels—enabling immediate, confident commercial decisions without leaving the platform.

---

## 2. Page Structure & Module Breakdown

### 2.1 Vendor Overview Header
- **Monogram & Visual Identity**: 2-letter uppercase monogram in high-contrast blue gradient.
- **Corporate Naming**: Commercial display name alongside official registered legal entity name (e.g. `Utkal GreenFiber Technologies Pvt. Ltd.`).
- **Location & Domain**: Precise city and country with verified link to the company's official domain.
- **Audit Badges**: Prominent `Demo Verified` or `Verified` badge with audit timestamp (`Audited November 2026`).
- **Score Indicators**:
  - **10-D Audit Score**: 98% (Cumulative compliance score across 10 deterministic checks).
  - **Data Confidence**: 95% (Evidence depth and source cross-referencing accuracy).

### 2.2 About this Vendor
- **Role & Specialization**: Clarifies whether the vendor is an OEM Manufacturer, Contract Packager, or Raw Material Producer.
- **Primary Industry**: Specific sector (e.g. Sustainable Packaging, Molded Fiber).
- **Manufacturing Capabilities**: Clean pill chips highlighting verified machinery lines (e.g. *High-density Molded Fiber*, *Contract Packaging*, *Custom Tooling*, *Bulk Export*).
- **Certifications**: Factual quality standards (e.g. *ISO 9001:2015*, *FSC Certified*, *CIPET Compostable ISO 17088*).

### 2.3 Products & Raw Materials Catalog
- Dedicated product cards showing:
  - Product title & category
  - Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)
  - Quoted Ex-Factory Unit Price
  - Estimated Landed Unit Cost
  - Direct *"Request Quote on this Product"* button pre-populating the RFQ modal.

### 2.4 Pricing & MOQ Commercial Panel
- Comprehensive pricing policy matrix:
  - Factual distinction between *Publicly Listed*, *Quoted*, and *Estimated*.
  - Strict compliance with the Truth-in-Data rule: If no public pricing exists, displays *"Price not publicly available. Request a quotation from this vendor."*

### 2.5 Location & Manufacturing Facilities
- Physical factory campus address (e.g. `Plot 42, Jagatpur Industrial Estate Phase II, Cuttack, Odisha 754021`).
- Exact GPS coordinates (`lat: 20.4952, lng: 85.9189`).
- Logistics corridor analysis: National Highway freight connections and rail container freight station (CFS) proximity.

### 2.6 Verified Public Business Contacts
- Strict privacy policy adherence: **No personal phone numbers or harvested private emails**.
- Public Business Email: `sales@utkalgreenfiber.com` (with one-click copy button).
- Official Business Phone: `+91 671 234 8920` (with one-click copy button).
- Verified Official Website.
- Primary CTA: *"Submit Formal RFQ"*.

### 2.7 10-Dimension Verification Dossier
Displays the granular evidence audit across all 10 deterministic checks:
1. **Corporate Identity & Registration**: Ministry of Corporate Affairs / Registrar of Companies.
2. **Official Website & Domain Ownership**: Whois and active HTTP domain validation.
3. **Product & Sourcing Relevance**: Verified catalog match against industrial packaging standards.
4. **Physical Factory Facility Geolocation**: Industrial estate registry and satellite geolocation verification.
5. **Legitimate Public Business Contacts**: Official domain-linked email and public switchboard check.
6. **Manufacturing Capabilities & Machinery**: Factory tooling audit and automated production line verification.
7. **Quality & Environmental Certifications**: ISO 9001, FSC, and CIPET accreditation proofs.
8. **Multi-Source Data Consistency**: Cross-referencing between trade registries and customs filings.
9. **Data Freshness & Telemetry**: Last checked within the current quarter.
10. **Financial & Compliance Sanctions**: Zero adverse regulatory orders or tax defaults.

### 2.8 AI Sourcing Fit & Intelligence
- Separate from factual verification: Provides AI compatibility analysis evaluated against the customer's specific business profile, target volumes, and budget constraints.
